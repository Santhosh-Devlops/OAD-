What happens to this file

Features are z-scored with the historical statistics (first 20 % of the rows), so streaming statistics stay causally valid.
The first 20 % train the SCD (Eq. 3–4) with Adam, then the IEC (Eq. 2, 5–8) and finally the DSD hypernetwork (Eq. 9–14) with gradients propagated through the SCD and the IEC.
The remaining 80 % are streamed one record at a time: uncertainty, escalation, score and threshold are recomputed for every record (Algorithm 2).
AUCROC/AUCPR, drift episodes and a full report are produced when the stream ends.

2 · Configure and run the pipeline

Defaults are the paper's settings (Adam lr 1e-2 decayed ×0.96, minibatch 64, |WN| = |WC| = 64, γ = 2, λ = 0.6, κ = 0.8, τ = 0.05, historical ratio 20 %).

SCD training — symmetric autoencoder, Adam, Eq. (3)–(4).
Pseudo-labelling + IEC — Eq. (5)–(8), repeated until μe converges.
